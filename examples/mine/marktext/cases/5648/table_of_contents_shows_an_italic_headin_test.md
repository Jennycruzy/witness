# Table of contents shows an italic heading without its Markdown asterisks

## Type italic heading
Press the keys #, Space, *, H, i, *, Enter

## Open table of contents
Press Ctrl+K to open the Table of Contents panel in the sidebar

## Verify TOC entry text
Verify the entry for the heading in the Table of Contents panel shows "Hi" with no asterisks, not "*Hi*"
