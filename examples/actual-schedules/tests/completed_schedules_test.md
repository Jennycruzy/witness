# Completed schedules are hidden again after leaving the page

## Open Schedules
Click "Schedules" in the sidebar.

## Complete the Rent schedule
Open the menu at the end of the "Rent" schedule's row and choose "Complete". The Rent schedule disappears from the list.

## Show completed schedules
Click "Show completed schedules". The Rent schedule is listed again, marked Completed.

## Leave and come back
Click "Budget" in the sidebar, then click "Schedules" in the sidebar again.

## Check completed schedules are hidden
Verify the Rent schedule is not listed and the "Show completed schedules" link is shown.
