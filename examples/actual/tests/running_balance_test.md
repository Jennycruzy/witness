# Running balance updates after editing a transaction

## Open the Wallet account
Click "Wallet" in the sidebar and wait for its transactions to show.

## Edit the deposit
Click the Deposit amount 20.00 of the transaction, change it to 50.00 and press Enter.

## Check the running balance
Verify the Balance column for the transaction shows 50.00.
