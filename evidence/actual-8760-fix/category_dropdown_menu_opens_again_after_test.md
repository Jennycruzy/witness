# Category dropdown menu opens again after renaming the category

## Open Food menu
On the Budget page, hover over the "Food" category row and click the dropdown arrow next to the name "Food"

## Choose Rename
In the menu that opens, click "Rename"

## Save new name
Clear the name field, type "Groceries" and press Enter

## Reopen category menu
Hover over the "Groceries" category row and click the dropdown arrow next to the name "Groceries"

## Check menu appears
Verify the dropdown menu next to the "Groceries" category is open and shows the option "Rename"
