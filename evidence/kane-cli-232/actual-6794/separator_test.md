# Amounts typed with the apostrophe thousands separator

## Open the Wallet account
Click "Wallet" in the sidebar and wait for its transactions to show.

## Add a deposit
Click "Add New", type 1'234.56 into the Deposit field of the new transaction and click "Add".

## Check the amount
Verify the new transaction's Deposit shows 1'234.56.
